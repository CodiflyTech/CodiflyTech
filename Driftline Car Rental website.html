Questions, answered

Still stuck? Our team replies in under five minutes, any hour.

Is the price I see the price I pay?

Yes. Taxes, fees and protection are included in the quote. Optional extras such as a child seat are shown before you confirm.

What do I need to pick up a car?

A full driving licence held for at least twelve months, the card used for the booking, and a passport or national ID. Some countries also need an International Driving Permit.

Is insurance included?

Basic protection is included in every quote. You can upgrade to full cover at checkout.

How late can I cancel?

Up to 48 hours before pick-up for a full refund.

How old do I need to be?

Drivers must be 21 or older. Drivers under 25 may pay a young-driver fee, which is included in the quote.
