Part 1: Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 2026

ARUMA PRODUCTS PRIVATE LIMITED ("we," "us," or "our") operates the EquaGrid: Math Logic mobile application (the "Service"). This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our Service and the choices you have associated with that data.

We are dedicated to safeguarding your privacy and ensuring your data safety. This document outlines how we comply with Google Play's Developer Policies, the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), and the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act of India.

1. Introduction

We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and disclose information when you use the EquaGrid: Math Logic application. By downloading or playing the game, you acknowledge and agree to the practices described in this policy.

2. Data Collection

To support game mechanics, resolve crashes, and serve advertisements, we collect and process specific classes of information:

  • Automatically Collected Data: Through integrated third-party SDKs (such as AdMob and Firebase Analytics), we may automatically collect your Advertising ID (GAID), IP address, and technical device metadata.
  • Game Progress: We store level progress, star ratings, mistakes count, and virtual currency balances (coins/hints) locally on your device (via Room database) and via encrypted cloud backups.
  • Financial Data: Purchase history and transaction tokens for In-App Purchases are processed securely via Google Play Billing. We do not store or collect credit card numbers or other payment methods.

3. Third-Party Services

The Service integrates third-party tools that may collect personal information used to identify you. These services process your data according to their respective privacy policies:

  • Google Play Services: Facilitates achievements, leaderboards, and cloud backup synchronization.
  • Google Ad Manager / AdMob: Delivers banner, interstitial, and rewarded video advertisements.
  • Firebase Analytics: Tracks general application stability, usage patterns, and diagnostic logs.
  • Google Play Billing: Manages transactions and restores user entitlements securely.

4. Children’s Privacy

This game is intended for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal data, please contact us immediately. If we discover that we have inadvertently collected data from a child under 13 without verified parental consent, we will take immediate steps to delete that information from our active databases.

In addition, if the game's targeting audience is configured to include children, we conform to the Google Play Families Policies by using Families Self-Certified Ads SDK settings and applying the appropriate tag for child-directed treatment to prevent unauthorized tracking.

5. Data Deletion & Rights

You have the right to request access to, correction of, or the permanent deletion of your personal data.

Users may request the deletion of their accounts and associated play data by contacting us directly at our support email address below. Upon verified request, we will purge all data from our active databases and inform integrated processors to do the same.
