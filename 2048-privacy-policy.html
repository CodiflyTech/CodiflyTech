Part 2: Comprehensive Privacy Policy

Introduction

At 2048 Match the Number, we believe that your privacy is a priority. This Privacy Policy describes how we handle information in our mobile application. Our app is designed to be a "local-only" experience, meaning your data stays where it belongs: on your device.

Information Collection and Use

We do not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information (PII).

  • No Personal Info: We do not ask for or collect names, emails, addresses, or phone numbers.
  • No Tracking: We do not use advertising IDs, analytics IDs, or any tracking SDKs.
  • No Media Access: We do not access your photos, files, camera, or microphone.

Local Data Storage

To provide core gameplay features, the app stores small amounts of data locally on your device using Android DataStore. This includes:

  • Game State: Your current tile positions so you can resume your game.
  • High Scores: Your personal best scores and "Best Tile" statistics.
  • User Preferences: Your choice regarding haptic feedback (vibration).

Note: This data is never uploaded to a server. If you clear the app cache or uninstall the app, this data is permanently removed.

App Permissions

We only request permissions that are essential for gameplay:

  • Haptic Feedback (Vibration): To provide tactile response when you merge tiles.
  • Internet Access: Included as a standard Android library requirement, but no user data is ever uploaded or downloaded.

Third-Party Services

Our app does not contain:

  • Third-party advertisements.
  • Third-party analytics (like Firebase or Google Analytics).
  • Social media integrations.

Children’s Privacy

2048 Match the Number is a math-based puzzle game suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children. Because we do not collect any data, we are fully compliant with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and international privacy laws.

Data Deletion Rights

You have the absolute right to manage and delete your data.

  1. In-App Reset: You can use the "Reset Statistics" button in the Stats screen or "Restart" in Settings to clear your scores.
  2. Uninstallation: Uninstalling the app from your device will automatically delete all locally stored game data.

Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes.
