Frequently Asked Questions

Still curious? Our journey specialists answer every inquiry within 24 hours.

Do I need a passport to begin planning?

A valid passport is required for all international expeditions. We verify visa requirements and validity windows during your initial consultation.

Can I fully customize my itinerary?

Yes. Every featured journey is a baseline that we tailor precisely to your dates, pace, accommodation style, and passions.

Are international flights included?

Flights are optional. We can seamlessly integrate private or commercial flights into your package or leave them for you to manage.

What is your cancellation policy?

We offer flexible booking windows with transparent terms outlined clearly in your custom proposal.

Will I have support during the trip?

Absolute peace of mind. A dedicated Codifly journey concierge is reachable 24/7 via private messaging and phone from departure to return.
